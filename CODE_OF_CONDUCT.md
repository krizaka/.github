# Code of Conduct

Krizaka projects adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

**Reporting.** Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the
[Krizaka contact form](https://www.krizaka.com/en/contact) (topic “Other”). Reports are reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially; maintainers may remove content and restrict participation, as
described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
